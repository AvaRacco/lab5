Montreal Trip

I went to Montreal with my sister, our friends, and our moms to go see Olivia Rodrigo. We decided to make a trip out of it and saw lots of cool things!

Notre-Dame Basilica

One of the highlights was seeing this church, the detail in the architecture was amazing. We went during the day and to the light show they put together which was also really cool.

The Unravelled Tour

My sister and our friends went to Olivia's night 2 show in Montreal and it was so much fun! The four of us have waited years to do this, we will never stop talking about being able to see her live.

I also may or may not have been noticed by the openers, I'm 99% sure I was.

Down-Town

There was lots of walking, but lots of fun places to go. We found a cool thrift store, book store, and some really good food.

When we were walking, I saw a Shepard Fairey mural which was a crazy coincidence because we just learned about him in class.
